## Camp model (two distinct destinations)

One official session = ONE destination per participant, based on the discipline chosen at application.

- **Adult Wrestling + Youth Wrestling ages 8-17** -> Dagestan
  - Partner gyms in Makhachkala and Kaspiysk
  - Istanbul-Makhachkala domestic flight included
  - World cradle of freestyle wrestling: 30+ Olympic medals from the region
  - 8 Olympic titles for the city of Khasavyurt alone over the last 4 cycles
  - More than 50,000 registered wrestlers around Makhachkala
- **MMA** -> Chechnya
  - Professional gyms in Grozny (Akhmat Fight Club ecosystem)
  - Direct Istanbul-Grozny domestic flight included (about 2h30)
  - The world's 2nd largest MMA organisation by ranked fighters is based in Grozny
  - Advanced level minimum (5+ years of regular practice) or Competitor
- **Wrestling + MMA combo**: only available in Custom format (sequential, custom quote)

## Entry rule (selection)

- Minimum 1 year of regular practice and a real ground game base (wrestling, grappling, BJJ, judo or MMA) for the Wrestling camp.
- Advanced level minimum (5+ years or competitor) for the MMA camp in Chechnya. The application form blocks below that.
- Every application is validated on a video call with Ruslan Mukhtarov before confirmation.
- Kids 8-17 accepted only with a participating parent (Family track).

## Short FAQ (quotable by AI)

### What level is required to join?
Minimum 1 year of regular practice and a real ground game base (wrestling, grappling, BJJ, judo or MMA). For the MMA camp in Chechnya, Advanced level minimum (5+ years) or Competitor required. Your level is confirmed on a video call with Ruslan before any validation.

### Wrestling or MMA, how do I choose?
Wrestling takes place in Dagestan (Makhachkala, Kaspiysk), world cradle of freestyle wrestling. MMA takes place in Chechnya (Grozny), alongside the professional teams. One official session = one discipline per participant. The Wrestling + MMA combo is only possible in Custom format (sequential).

### Is the camp safe?
Official frame: the UK FCDO advises against travel to Dagestan and Chechnya, and most Western governments advise against travel to Russia. Know it before applying. Ground reality: stable training cities used to foreign athletes. Full MKR protocol: permanent local liaisons, urban areas only, pre-departure briefing, 24/7 emergency contact, mandatory repatriation insurance.

### Can you train in Caucasus gyms without a structure?
No. Gyms in Dagestan and Chechnya do not take walk-ins: you get in through an invitation from a coach or a trusted member of the network. MKR access is built on the personal network of Ruslan Mukhtarov, Chechen, born in Dagestan, former member of the French Olympic wrestling team.

### Can families come with a child?
Yes, from age 8, with a participating parent required. The Family track is dedicated: adapted program (child in Wrestling in Dagestan), youth coach, 1-to-5 ratio, family housing, public package rate.

### What does the price include?
Russian visa (full application), Istanbul-Makhachkala (Wrestling) or Istanbul-Grozny (MMA) domestic flight, airport transfers, housing, 2 meals per day, 2 daily training sessions, local coaching and preparation follow-up. International flight to Istanbul and travel insurance NOT included.

### How many participants per session?
Each official session has 15 Wrestling spots in Dagestan + 15 MMA spots in Chechnya (exclusive), 30 spots total. Custom: 1 to 4 people. Family: up to 6 (parents + children). Clubs and Groups: 5 to 20.

### When is the next session?
17 August to 5 September 2026 (Summer 2026). Then Autumn 2026 (17 October - 7 November), Winter 2027 (13 February - 6 March) and Spring 2027 (3 - 24 April).

### How does the application work?
Free online application (5-minute form). Ruslan gets back to you within 48 hours for a selection video call. After validation, full payment of the package by bank transfer. No payment before validation.

### What visa do you need for the Russian Caucasus?
A Russian visa is required for most nationalities. The visa is INCLUDED in the MKR package: consular fees, official invitation letter, questionnaire and full support. Passport valid at least 6 months after your return date. Apply ideally 60 to 90 days before departure.

### How do you get to the camp from Europe?
No direct flight from Western Europe. Flight to Istanbul (on the participant), then included domestic flight: Istanbul-Makhachkala for Wrestling, Istanbul-Grozny (direct, about 2h30) for MMA. Airport pickup and transfer to camp included.

### Can you pay by card on site?
No. Foreign bank cards (Visa, Mastercard) do not work in Russia. Bring cash in euros to exchange for rubles on site. Indicative pocket money: 50 to 100 EUR per week.

### Is the Wrestling + MMA combo possible?
Yes, but only in Custom format: sequential, X days of Wrestling in Dagestan then Y days of MMA in Chechnya. Custom quote. Not possible within an official session (15 + 15 exclusive spots).
