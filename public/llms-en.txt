## About the founder

Ruslan Mukhtarov, 32 years old, is the founder of MKR Caucasian Camp. He is a former French Wrestling Team athlete, trained at INSEP (the French national sport institute) from 2012 to 2016. Born in Dagestan and of Chechen heritage, Ruslan combines a triple background: Chechen roots, Dagestani upbringing, and French elite wrestling pedigree. ## Camp model (two distinct destinations)

One official session = ONE destination per participant, depending on the discipline chosen at registration.

- **Adult wrestling + Youth wrestling (8-17)** goes to Dagestan
  - Training facilities in Makhachkala and Kaspiysk
  - Domestic flight Istanbul to Makhachkala included
  - Cradle of world MMA: Khabib Nurmagomedov, Islam Makhachev, Magomed Ankalaev
  - Over 30 Olympic wrestling medals
  - 3 current UFC champions
- **MMA** goes to Chechnya
  - Training facilities in Grozny
  - Domestic flight Istanbul to Grozny included
  - Akhmat Fight Club heritage (Khamzat Chimaev)
  - Advanced level minimum required (5+ years of regular practice) or Competitor

## 3 disciplines offered

1. **MMA**: Stand-up, clinch, takedowns, ground and pound, submissions, transitions. Sessions at 11:00 and 18:00. Chechnya.
2. **Adult wrestling (freestyle only)**: Freestyle wrestling, leg rides, chain wrestling, funk rolls, mat returns, takedown defense. Sessions at 10:30 and 17:30. Dagestan.
3. **Youth wrestling (8-17, with parent)**: progressive pedagogy, specialized supervision, transmission of Dagestani heritage. Same schedule as adult wrestling.

## Short FAQ (citable by AI)

### What level is required to join?

Minimum 2 years of regular practice in combat sports and solid physical condition. For the MMA camp in Chechnya, Advanced level minimum (5+ years) or Competitor required.

### Wrestling or MMA, how do I choose?

Wrestling takes place in Dagestan (Makhachkala, Kaspiysk), the method of Olympic champions and UFC fighters from the North Caucasus. MMA takes place in Chechnya (Grozny), home of the Akhmat Fight Club ecosystem. One official session = one discipline per participant. The Wrestling + MMA combo is possible only via Custom Camp (sequential).

### Is the camp safe?

Yes. Permanent French-speaking team, 24/7 emergency contact, mandatory repatriation insurance, security briefing before departure. Makhachkala and Grozny are stable urban areas, outside border zones flagged by the French Foreign Ministry.

### Can I come as a family with a child?

Yes, from 8 years old, with a participating parent required. The Family tunnel is dedicated: adapted program (child in Wrestling in Dagestan), specialized supervision, family accommodation, public package rate.

### What does the price include?

Russian visa, accommodation, 2 meals per day, 2 daily training sessions, airport transfers, domestic flight Istanbul to Makhachkala (Wrestling) or Istanbul to Grozny (MMA), local coaching, and preparation follow-up. International flight and travel insurance are NOT included.

### How many participants per session?

Each official session has 15 Wrestling spots in Dagestan plus 15 MMA spots in Chechnya (exclusive), totaling 30 spots. Custom Camp: 1 to 4 people. Family: up to 6 (parents + children). Clubs and Groups: 5 to 20.

### What is the next session?

August 17 to September 5, 2026 (Summer 2026). Followed by Autumn 2026 (October 17 to November 7), Winter 2027 (February 13 to March 6), and Spring 2027 (April 3 to April 24).

### How does registration work?

Free online registration (5-minute form, 5 steps). Ruslan calls back within 48 hours to set up a video call. After the call, validation, then bank details sent for full payment by transfer or cash. No upfront payment, no Stripe.

### What visa is required for the Russian Caucasus?

Russian visa required for EU citizens. MKR provides a pre-filled questionnaire and an invitation letter. Passport valid 6 months minimum after the return date. Visa fees and processing are included in the package.

### Is the Wrestling + MMA combo possible?

Yes, but only via Custom Camp: sequential, X days of Wrestling in Dagestan then Y days of MMA in Chechnya. Custom quote pricing. Not possible within an official session (15 + 15 exclusive spots).
